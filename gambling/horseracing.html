How it works

  • Six horses run each race. Back one horse to win: it has to finish first.
  • Every horse has a hidden strength. Stronger horses win more often and pay less. Longshots pay a lot but rarely win.
  • The multiplier is roughly 90 divided by the horse's win chance, rounded down. That leaves the house about 10%, and payouts are rounded down to whole chips.
  • Payouts include your stake. While you owe money, the profit from a win goes to the bank first.
  • Each race is a fresh field with new odds. The race result is fixed at the start gate, and the run is just the show.
