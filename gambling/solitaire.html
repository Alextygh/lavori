House rules

  • This is Vegas-style Klondike. Your buy-in is taken when you deal.
  • Each card you send to the foundations pays 5% of your buy-in, so you need 20 cards home to break even. Clearing all 52 pays 2.6 times your buy-in. Payouts are rounded down when you cash out.
  • Turn up 1 card at a time from the stock. The cards always come in the same order, and you may go through the stock 5 times.
  • Build tableau columns down in alternating colors. Only a King (with any cards on it) can fill an empty column.
  • Foundations build up from Ace to King in a single suit. Cards on a foundation cannot be moved back, so the money is locked in.
  • Double-click (or double-tap) a card to send it automatically to a place where it fits: its foundation first, otherwise a tableau column.
  • You can also tap a card to select it (this takes the cards on top of it too), then tap where it should go.
  • Press Cash Out whenever you are stuck. Your hand is saved if you leave, and the result is only paid when you cash out or clear the board.
  • While you owe money, the profit from a win goes to the bank first.

Your chips are saved in this browser and shared with the main menu. Out of chips? Only the main menu loan can save you, and it comes with debt.
