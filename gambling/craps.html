House rules

  • Tap a bet area to place the selected chip on it. The chips leave your balance right away.
  • Come-out roll: on the Pass Line, 7 or 11 wins and 2, 3 or 12 loses. Any other number becomes the point. Then the shooter keeps rolling: hitting the point again wins, and a 7 loses.
  • Don't Pass is the opposite. 2 or 3 wins, 7 or 11 loses, 12 is a push (your bet is returned), and after a point it wins on a 7 and loses if the point repeats.
  • Pass and Don't Pass pay 1 to 1. You can only hold one of the two, and they are locked in once a point is set.
  • Odds open once a point is set, up to 2 times your line bet, and pay true odds with no house edge. Pass odds pay 2 to 1 on 4 or 10, 3 to 2 on 5 or 9, and 6 to 5 on 6 or 8. Don't Pass odds pay the reverse (1 to 2, 2 to 3, 5 to 6). Payouts are rounded down to whole chips.
  • Field is a one-roll bet. 3, 4, 9, 10 or 11 pays 1 to 1, 2 or 12 pays 2 to 1, and anything else loses.
  • Any 7 is a one-roll bet that pays 4 to 1 if the next roll is a 7.
  • Every roll is saved the moment it happens, and your table is kept if you leave. While you owe money, the profit from a roll goes to the bank first.
