How it works

  • This copies the structure of the real Powerball: 5 white balls from 69 and 1 red Powerball from 26. The odds are the real ones, so the jackpot is almost impossible to win.
  • Prizes are scaled to chips. Your ticket costs 5 chips.
  • Everything is drawn the moment you press Buy and Draw. Each draw, the jackpot grows as if other players were buying tickets. If someone (you) wins it, it resets.
  • Lottery tickets lose money over time, and this one is built to show that. While you owe money, the profit from any win goes to the bank first.
