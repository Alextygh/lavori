How it works

  • This is 75-ball bingo. Each card is a 5×5 grid with columns B (1-15), I (16-30), N (31-45), G (46-60) and O (61-75). The centre square is free.
  • All 35 balls are drawn the moment you press Play, then called one at a time. Matching numbers on your cards light up.
  • One card can complete several lines at once, and you are paid for the total. A card with a single line gets 10 chips, so it just doubles your stake.
  • Odds and prizes come from a simulation of millions of cards, so they are close but not exact. Over time you get back about 89% of what you spend, which is how real games stay profitable for the house.
  • While you owe money, the profit from any win goes to the bank first.
