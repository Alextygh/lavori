How it works

  • Each card has nine hidden amounts. Scratch the cells with your finger or mouse. If three of them match, you win that amount.
  • Four cards to choose from. The prizes scale with the price, so a 50 chip card can pay up to 200 times 50.
  • The card is printed the moment you buy it, so scratching more slowly or faster changes nothing. You can also press Reveal All.
  • Your prize is paid when the card is fully revealed. If you leave with a card half scratched, it waits for you here and you cannot buy another until you reveal it.
  • While you owe money, the profit from a win goes to the bank first.
