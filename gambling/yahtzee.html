How it works

  • Standard solo Yahtzee. Each round you roll five dice up to three times, tap dice to hold them, then score the roll in one empty box.
  • Upper section: boxes for ones to sixes score the sum of that number. Reach 63 points there for a 35 point bonus.
  • Lower section: 3 of a kind and 4 of a kind score all five dice, Full House 25, Small Straight 30, Large Straight 40, Yahtzee 50, Chance scores all five dice.
  • A second Yahtzee after scoring 50 in the Yahtzee box adds 100, and then follows the usual joker rule: use the matching upper box if it is empty, otherwise the roll counts as a Full House, Small Straight or Large Straight in full.
  • The entry fee is taken when you start. If you leave in the middle of a game, it waits for you here, dice and score sheet included.
  • While you owe money, the profit from a win goes to the bank first.
