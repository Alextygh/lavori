OPERATOR NOTES
CRISES
Crises appear in the right panel during play. Each has a countdown timer and three response options.

Only one option resolves the crisis correctly. The others make things worse.

A correct answer repairs the affected system and earns points. A wrong answer damages the system and injures a random resident.

If the timer hits zero the crisis expires - full system damage, no points.
GREEN timer: plenty of time
ORANGE timer: hurry up
RED timer: critical - act now
Try clicking the buttons on the crises to the left. See what happens when you pick correctly or wrongly.
RESIDENTS
Five people live in the bunker. Each has an HP bar that depletes from bad crisis decisions, failing systems, or radiation.

If all residents die the mission fails.

Watch their border color:
ORANGE pulse: HP below 35%, in danger
BLUE pulse: personal crisis active - click them immediately to open a minigame
Minigame success gives +25 HP and score. Failure deals -30 HP.

Crises keep ticking while a minigame is open.
SYSTEMS
Six systems keep the bunker alive. They all decay slowly and crises can accelerate the decline.

Five systems must stay HIGH: Oxygen, Food, Power, Water, Morale.

Radiation must stay LOW. Its bar fills as danger rises - the opposite of all others.

Below 15% on any vital system: residents take constant passive damage every second.
Above 60% radiation: all residents are irradiated each second until sealed.
Correct crisis answers repair the relevant system. Wrong answers damage it further.
MINIGAMES
Each resident has a unique personal crisis minigame that opens when you click them while they are blue-glowing.

All minigames are timed. Fail the minigame or run out of time: the resident loses 30 HP.

Success: +25 HP to resident, bonus score
Failure: -30 HP, possible death
Abandoning via the button counts as failure. Crises keep their timers while the minigame is active - only the crisis answer buttons are locked.
WAVES AND WIN
Survive 10 waves to complete the mission. Each wave lasts 40 seconds.

Each new wave makes crises spawn faster, shrink timers, and increases the chance of simultaneous crises.

Score multiplies with wave number - high-wave, fast resolutions earn the most points.

Lose when: all residents die, or any vital system hits 0%
