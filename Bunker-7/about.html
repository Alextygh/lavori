ABOUT THIS PROGRAM
BUNKER-7
CRISIS MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
VERSION 6.0 | DEEP SHELTER PROTOCOL
BUNKER-7 is a real-time crisis management game set in an underground shelter.

Manage five residents, keep six critical systems alive, and survive wave after wave of emergencies.

Each resident has a unique personal minigame triggered when their emergency light activates.

The game escalates with every wave. Crises spawn faster, timers shrink, and multiple emergencies strike simultaneously.
