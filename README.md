
# About this

Hello to you who are reading this, I'm Ale. I am quite bored, so I do a lot of web pages, usually about things that are not really useful. Consider me a knockoff of [Neal.fun](https://neal.fun) (Props to that guy.)

So yeah, some of them are school work, really boring ones that I must do. They are not even in English, so don't go look at them, otherwise, hello Professor! Hope you're having an amazing day.

If you want to visit my web pages, you can go directly to the [index](https://alextygh.github.io/lavori).

**Note**: A couple of them will be with "**--External Website--**". This will mean that even though they are in the index, they will not be on GitHub and so you will not be able to see their codes.






## Pages
Here, in all of it's glory, its every page you can visit (except school works and betas) and their functionality in the order that they appear on the index:

### [EVERYTHING](https://alextygh.github.io/lavori/EVERYTHING.html)
On this website it's impossible to get the same page twice. You have 6 widgets, each of them is random, and each random widget has a random value inside of it. You could get one that tells you the time in London, one that gives you the temperature in Cape Verde, chess openings, dice rolls, words etymologies, songs, movie, books, recipes, tarot cards, you get where I am going, even the favicon and titles are random, the fonts are random (there is Comic Sans). Try to get to the exact same page twice. I'll give you $100.

### [Bunker-7](https://alextygh.github.io/lavori/Bunker-7/index.html)
This is a little game about where you manage your bunker. You are the overseer and have to manage crises and your residents' problems. It's not that long of a game but it can be very hard. (There are achievements!)

You can also play it on [itch.io](https://ale-11011.itch.io/bunker-7).

### [Character Compendium](https://alextygh.github.io/lavori/Characters%20Compendium/hub.html)
Here you can look up (as of now) every character from Marvel, DC and Star Wars, just choose the universe, type the character and hit enter. It will list every instance of that character, and its biography, all related characters and whatnot. The info is taken directly from their fandom ([Marvel](https://marvel.fandom.com/), [DC](https://dc.fandom.com/), [Star Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/)). It is updated in real time, so you can probably even see brand-new characters from the new Spider-Man movie or something.

### [Character Questionnaire](https://alextygh.github.io/lavori/character-questionnaire-it.html)
I don't think this is useful for you. You can create your original character with this. It's just a questionnaire, so it will help you with inspiration more than actually creating a character, for that use your own imagination.

### [Chessverse](https://alextygh.github.io/lavori/Chess/index.html)
With this you can play chess against famous (and old) chess engines, you can even play against me! You can make them play against each other. Each opponent has their own custom chessboard and they play like their original counterpart. They are advanced chess engines so they are pretty tough to beat.

**For Nerds**: The engines use Stockfish, they copy the style and difficulty from a Lichess game where they played against me.

### [Cosmic Realty](https://alextygh.github.io/lavori/cosmic-realty.html)

Here you can buy planets. Yes, actually, if you have some quadrillion Galactic Credits and 3-5 millenia to wait, you can definitely buy one. ### [Duckapedia](https://alextygh.github.io/lavori/Duckapedia/index.html)
An encyclopedia of rubber ducks from the game Hitman: World of Assassination. This is *outdated* but it displays every duck in the game up to June 2026. You can find all information about every duck up to that point. I have no plan of keeping it up to date as of now.

### [Emergency Map](https://alextygh.github.io/lavori/emergency-map.html)
This map powered by OpenStreetMap lets you choose a location on the map (or use your device's GPS), select the emergency type you need, and it tells you how much time it would take for each selected emergency type to reach your location. It locates hospitals, fire stations and police stations up to 20km, and it shows the route from your position and the closest's emergency station and the approximate time.

### [Lucky Sim](https://alextygh.github.io/lavori/gambling/index.html)
A simulator made only for entrainment that simulates real-life gambling games. There are games such as Poker, Blackjack, Roulette, Horse-racing and much more! This is only a simulation and does not use any real money, it does show how gambling can make you lose so much. Please do not gamble. 

### [Pokémon](https://pkm-stats-game.netlify.app/)
